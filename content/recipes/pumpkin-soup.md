Silky, warming, and finished with a swirl of cream and seeds.


## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion
- 1 garlic
- 1 potato
- 2 carrots
- 200g pumpkin
- lovor
- salt
- cumin
- cooking cream

## Steps
1. Fry shortly diced onions and carrots.
2. Add one diced potato and cleaned pumpkin.
3. Cover with boiled water and boil over medium heat, until vegetables soften.
4. Add lovor in the water as well.
5. After veggies are cooked, blend everything with stick mixer.
6. Add spices according to your preferences.
7. Add one small piece of butter and some cooking cream.
8. Mix until beautiful.
9. Serve with toasted slice of bread.
10. Enjoy!
