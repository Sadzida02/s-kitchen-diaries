A layered Middle Eastern dish that is crunchy, creamy and comforting at once.

## Ingredients

- 3 or 4 tortillas
- Ground beef
- onions
- carrot
- tomato sauce
- cumin
- garam masala
- salt
- pepper
- garlic
- eggplants
- yogurt
- tahin paste

## Steps

- Cut tortilas in triangles and coat with some oil.
- Bake in the oven until crsipy.
- Dice up onion and carrots, fry and add beef, spices and tomato sauce. 
- Cook over medium heat.
- Add some water, cover and cook some more.
- Wash eggplants, cut in slices anc cover in salt.
- After 10 mins, rinse up and dice uo into small cubes.
- Bake it in the oven or fry in the pan.
- In a tray layer tortillas, beef and eggplants.
- Top wtih yogurt mixed with tsp of tahin paste and enjoy.
