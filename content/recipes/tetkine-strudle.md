A homemade strudel from my aunt's recipe. Sweet, soft and perfect with tea.

## Ingredients
- 1 egg
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 cup oil
- 1 cup milk
- 3 to 4 cups flour
- 1 pack baking powder
- jam of your choice (bestilj is the best)

## Steps

- Form the dough and divide in 5 equal-sized balls.
- Roll out each ball into rectangular shape.
- Spread jam over the dough and roll it like cinammon rolls.
- Make 5 rolls and place them in a tray.
- Bake in the oven and dust with some powdered sugar.
