Healty treat when you crave something sweet.

## Ingredients
- 1 egg
- 40g dates
- 60ml of milk
- 1 tsp yogurt
- 40g spelt flour
- 15g oats
- 1/2 tsp pf baking powder
- cinammon
- salt
- greek yogurt
- pekmez

## Steps

- Soak dates into warm milk ans blend with stick blender.
- Add oats and mix again.
- Add rest of ingredients.
- Heat up the pan to high temperature. 
- U don't need oil, if u heat it up properly.
- Prepare over medium heat.
- Top with yogurt, pekmez and granola.
- Enjoy!
