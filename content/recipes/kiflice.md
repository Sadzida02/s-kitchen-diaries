Soft, golden rolls from my own kitchen.

## Ingredients
- 10g of yeast
- 2tsp sugar
- 60ml oil
- 4 cups of flour
- 300-400ml warm water

## Steps
- After making the dough leave it to rest for an hour.
- Prepare filling with cheese, or like pizza with salama, ketchup and oregano.
- You can also fill them with sweet jam or cream.
- Make two balls, and roll out each in large circles.
- Cut in up to 20 triangles.
- Press edges nicely while rolling so that filling does not go out and burn
- Brush them gently with whipped egg and sprinkle with some seeds like sesame.
- Bake for 20ish minutes at 220 degrees Celsius.
- Enjoy!
