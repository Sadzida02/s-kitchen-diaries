Fresh grated carrots in a simple, bright dressing.

## Ingredients

- 4 carrots
- 1tsp honey
- raisins
- 1tbsp whipped cream
- yogurt
- salt
- pepper

## Steps
- Grate carrots and put in a bowl.
- Add honey, raisins.
- Pour some yogurt and cream.
- If too thick add some more yogurt.
- Season with salt and papper acording to taste.
- Enjoy!
