Soft, chocolatey muffins, easy to make and easy to share.

## Ingredients
- 1 egg
- 130g sugar
- 1 vanilla sugar
- 90ml oil
- 250ml milk
- 250g flour
- 3 tbsp of cocoa powder
- 1/2 tbsp of baking soda
- 1 pack of baking powder
- salt
- 150g of chocolate
- frozne fruits

## Steps
- Mix eggs and sugar with mixer.
- Add other ingredients.
- Dice up chocolate and add some frozen fruits.
- Bake and enjoy!
