The most beautiful smell of Ashure in month of Muharrem.

## Ingredients

- 7 to 77 ingredients of your choice- (fruits (dried or fresh), spme veggies, nuts, grains, rice, sugar, spices, water)

## Steps

- Cook grains separately
- Cook some veggies
- Dice up most of the fruit
- Chop up all the chunky pieces
- in big pot add some water and sugar and boil it with dried fruits
- Keep adding other ingredients
- Mix and add water
- Enjoy!
