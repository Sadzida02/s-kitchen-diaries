Tender chicken in a creamy, gently spiced tomato sauce, best with rice or warm bread.

## Ingredients

- 2 pieces of chicken breast
- 1,5dl of yoghurt
- 2 tsp of garam masala
- 1 tsp of caraway
- 2 tsp of turmeric
- curry
- salt
- 2 onions
- garlic
- chilli flakes
- ginger
- 1 tomato
- 1 tsp of cardamom
- 1 tsp of cinammon
- 3 dl water
- 1.5 dl of cream

## For the rice
- 1 cube of butter
- 1 cup of rice
- 1,5 cups of water
- salt
- raisins
- walnuts
- almonds


## Steps

- marinate chicken in yogurt and spices and leave to rest for few hours in fridge.
- Fry onions and spices.
- Add tomatoes and pour in sepaarte bowl.
- Blend with stick mixer.
- In the same pan fry chicken and take it out.
- Put the sauce back and add some more water.
- Cook over some medium heat.
- Add whipped cream and chicken.
- Cook a litte more so eveything blends in

## For the rice
- Melt butter and boiled water.
- Rinse up rice and add to boiling water.
- Cook on lowest heat for 12 minutes.
- Don't take off the lead whole time. 
- Then turn off the heat, and leave covered for 10 more mins.
- Garnish with nuts and dried fruit.
- Enjoy!
