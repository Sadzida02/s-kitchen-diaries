Thin chocolate crepes filled with yoghurt and  raspberries.

## Ingredients

- 2 eggs
- 1 tsp of sugar
- pinch of salt
- 1 cup of milk
- 1 cup of mineral water
- flour
- 2 tbsp of cocoa
- raspberry jam
- greek yoghurt

## Steps
- Make usual pancake batter, but with cocoa powder.
- After prepping pancakes, spread raspberry jam and yoghurt and roll them up nicely.
- Enjoy!
