When you crave taste of the fall, this healthy combination of apples and peanut butter can help to satisfy it.

## Ingredients
- 1 apple
- 35 g oats
- 1og honey
- 3g chia
- cinammon
- 1 tbsp of peanut butter
- almonds
- walnuts

## Steps

- Cook oats on low, until thickens up.
- Top with sliced apple, peanut butter and nuts.
- Enjoy!
