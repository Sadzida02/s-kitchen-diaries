Baked in the oven and served warm, straight from the tray.

## Ingredients
- 1 onion
- carrots
- 1 chicken breasts
- frozen vegetables (2 packs, carsko mix, cauliflower, brussel sprouts)
- 300ml of milk
- 2 spoons of flour
- salt
- pepper
- cheese

## Steps

- Dice up onions and carrots and fry it in the pan.
- Add diced chicken and spice up with spices of choice.
- Pepper, salt, garam masala, oregano.
- In a pot boil some water and add vegetables.
- Boil it for 5-10 mins.
- Prepare beschamel sauce, by adding butter to another pot. Add flour and milk and stir until the liquid thickens up.
- Take a casserole dish and layer up first chicken, then boiled vegetables and then sauce.
- Finally grate some cheese on top and add some parsley.
- Bake for 20-30 mins at 250 degrees Celsius.
