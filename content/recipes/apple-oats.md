When you crave taste of the fall, this healthy combination of apples and cinammon can help to satisfy it.

## Ingredients
- 1 apple
- 35 g oats
- 1og honey
- 3g chia
- cinammon
- 1 tbsp of greek yoghurt
- pekmez

## Steps

- Stir fry diced apples with optional butter, and then add honey and cinammon. Then add water and oats, cook on low, until thickens up.
- Top with a spoon of grek yoghurt, seeds and pekmez
- Enjoy!
