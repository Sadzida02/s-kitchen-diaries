A breakfast that tastes like dessert.


## Ingredients
## Ingredients for one portion
- 35g of rolled oats
- cinammon
- chia seeds
- cacao powder
- 1/2 banana
- greek yogurt
- honey

## Steps
- Cook oats, chia, and cinamon with water until it thicknes up.
- Mash banana with coccoa.
- Mix yogurt with teaspoon of honey.
- Sprea chocolate mixture on one half, and white part on the other half.
- Top with some seeds or granola.
- Enjoy!
