Viral healthy pudding, which on first glance doesn't promise, but on second is suprisingly delicious and amazing.

## Ingredients
- 4 boiled eggs
- 1/2 of avocado
- 1 tbsp of honey
- some milk
- splash of vanilla



## Steps
- In blender add all the ingredients.
- Blend until extra smooth, no chunks in sight.
- If too thick add some milk.
- Top with some berries or eat like so.
- Enjoy!
