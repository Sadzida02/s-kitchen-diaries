## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion
- 1 garlic
- 1 potato
- 2 carrots
- 200g pumpkin
- lovor
- salt
- cumin
- cooking cream

## Steps
1. Mix everything together.
2. Bake until golden.
