A soft, spiced carrot cake that is hard to stop at one slice.

## Ingredients for cake
- 4 eggs
- 200g sugar
- 200ml oil
- 250g flour 
- 1 pack of backing powder
- 100g of walnuts
- 500g of carrots
- cinammon

## Ingredients for cream

- 300g of cream cheese
- 1 vanilla sugar
- 200ml of whipped cream
- 50g of powdered sugar

## Steps

- Whip up eggs with suggar 
- Add oil, flour and powder.
- Grate carrots and add them to mixture and spices.
- Bake for 30-40 minutes, at 180 celsius degrees.
- Prepare cream cheese topping, while cake bakes.
- If u have extra time, put cream in the pipping bag and top the cake with small dollaps.
- You can add some zest on top optionally.
