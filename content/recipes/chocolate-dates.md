Dark chocolate, a crunch of pistachio, and a little romance on the plate.

## Ingredients

- Tasty dates
- chcolate
- peanut butter
- almonds

## For decorations:
- roses
- pistachios
- white chocolate

## Steps
- Melt chocolate.
- Stuff dates with almonds and peanut butter.
- Dip filled dates in chocolate.
- Top wit the decor of your choice.
