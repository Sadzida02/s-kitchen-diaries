A traditional pie with a savoury green filling, a Bosnian classic.

## Ingredients

- 1 pack of jufke
- lots of spinach
- cow cheese
- 1 egg
- 2 tbsp of sour cream
- salt

## Ingredients for the liquid dough
- 4 spoons of flour
- 10g of yeast
- salt
- 1 cup of milk
- 1/2 cup of oil

## Steps
- Wash spoinach, cleand and cut it up nicely.
- Add egg, cheese, cream and salt to spinach.
- In a separate bowl mix the flour mixture.
- Spread with a brush mixture equally on each sheet of jufka.
- Put some spinach filling.
- Fold edges and roll each sheet.
- Bake until perfect.
- Enjoy!
